01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

NOT JUST
AN EDIT.

Hey, I'm Nikhil Shinde, the creator behind EditingHub. I enjoy working with footage, music, timing, transitions, and visual effects to build edits that feel energetic, polished, and memorable.

My main focus is cinematic car edits, speed ramps, short-form videos, motion graphics, and video shooting. I love experimenting with creative ideas and finding new ways to make each project stand out.

I use Adobe After Effects and Photoshop in my creative workflow. Every project gives me an opportunity to improve my technique, refine my style, and learn something new.

My goal is to grow as a professional video editor and visual creator, collaborate with clients and creators, and build work that people remember.
